Q1 "Are you listed on pmsuryaghar.gov.in as an approved vendor for Andhra Pradesh?"

This is the single most important question you can ask. The PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana subsidy, up to ₹78,000, can only be claimed if your installation is done through a vendor who is officially registered on the pmsuryaghar.gov.in portal as an approved installer for your state. If the vendor is not on that list, you will not receive a single rupee of subsidy, regardless of what they promise you.

🔍 How to verify in 60 seconds: Go to pmsuryaghar.gov.in → click "Apply for Rooftop Solar" → enter your state as Andhra Pradesh → search for the vendor's name or company registration. If they do not appear, they are not approved.

🚩 Red Flag Answer

"We will handle everything, don't worry about the portal.", Without being listed, they physically cannot process your subsidy. This is a lie or an admission of disqualification.

✅ Right Answer

"Yes, here is our vendor ID on the portal, you can verify it yourself right now." A legitimate company will be proud to show you their listing immediately.

Why it matters: Installing with a non-approved vendor means you pay full price with zero subsidy, effectively overpaying by ₹60,000 to ₹78,000 compared to someone who chose correctly.

Q2 "Do the solar panels you will install appear on the MNRE's ALMM list?"

The ALMM (Approved List of Models and Manufacturers) is a list maintained by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy of solar panel brands and models that meet India's quality and performance standards. Installing panels that are not on this list is one of the most common ways homeowners in Andhra Pradesh lose their subsidy eligibility without knowing it until it is too late.

When APEPDCL conducts its final commissioning inspection, one of the first things they check is whether your panels are on the ALMM list. If they are not, the inspection fails, the subsidy application is rejected, and you are stuck with panels that nobody will officially certify.

🔍 How to verify: Ask the vendor for the make and model of the panels they plan to install. Then search for that brand and model on the MNRE website under "ALMM List." Any reputable company will give you this information without hesitation.

🚩 Red Flag Answer

"These are high-quality imported panels, better than what's on the list.", No panel, however expensive or imported, qualifies for the subsidy unless it is ALMM-listed. This is a serious warning sign.

✅ Right Answer

"We use [Brand Name], Model [XYZ], which is on the ALMM list. Here is the certificate." They should be able to show you the documentation instantly.

Why it matters: Non-ALMM panels are often significantly cheaper, which is why some vendors offer low quotes. But those "savings" disappear the moment your APEPDCL inspection fails and your subsidy gets rejected.

Q3 "Will you give me a complete itemised written quote, right now, before I pay anything?"

A verbal quote means nothing. A vague quote that just says "3 kW system, ₹1,40,000" means almost nothing. What you need before parting with a single rupee is a written quote that lists every component separately, panel brand and wattage, inverter brand and model, mounting structure type, cable specifications, installation charge, earthing and lightning protection, net meter application charge, and what is not included.

Hidden charges are one of the most common complaints against solar companies across India. The quote looks reasonable, then comes the "surprise" charge for wiring, or the roof drilling, or the DISCOM application fee, or the "quality panel upgrade" that was apparently not in the original package.

🚩 Red Flag Answer

"I'll send it later after you confirm." / "The quote depends on factors we check at installation.", Any company that cannot give you a full written quote today is either disorganised or hiding something.

✅ Right Answer

They hand you or email you a complete itemised quote, on company letterhead, with GST breakup, payment schedule, and a clear list of what is included, the same day as your enquiry.

Why it matters: A transparent written quote is the first real test of a company's integrity. If they are honest about pricing before you pay, they are likely to be honest about everything else too. If they are not, run.

Q7 "Are you GSTIN registered in Andhra Pradesh, and will you provide a GST invoice?"

This question sounds like a boring accounting detail, but it is anything but. A solar company that is not GST-registered in Andhra Pradesh is operating informally. That means no legal invoice, no tax compliance trail, no formal record of your purchase. If they disappear tomorrow, you have almost no legal recourse.

A GST invoice also matters practically: it is required documentation for the PM Surya Ghar subsidy process. Without a proper GST invoice from a registered vendor, your subsidy application will be incomplete and potentially rejected.

🔍 How to verify: Ask for their GSTIN number and check it instantly at gst.gov.in/searchtaxpayer. A valid AP-registered business will have a GSTIN beginning with 37 (AP state code). Telangana begins with 36.

🚩 Red Flag Answer

"We can give you a cash receipt." / "GST invoice will add extra cost.", This means they are not GST-compliant. They are not a formally registered business. Do not proceed.

✅ Right Answer

"Yes, our GSTIN for AP is [number]. You can verify it on gst.gov.in right now, and all invoices are GST-compliant." Immediate, transparent, confident.

Why it matters: A GST-registered company is a real, compliant, traceable business. If something goes wrong, with the installation, the warranty, the subsidy, you have a legal paper trail to protect you.

Q8 "What exactly does your warranty cover, and who actually honours it if you close down?"

Every solar company in Visakhapatnam will say "25-year warranty", because that sounds impressive and they know it is what you want to hear. But there are three very different warranties in solar, and most salespeople either do not explain the difference or actively confuse them.

The product warranty covers manufacturing defects in the panel itself, typically 10 to 12 years from the panel manufacturer. The performance warranty guarantees the panel will still produce a minimum percentage of rated power after 25 years, typically 80 to 85%. The workmanship warranty covers the installation, wiring, mounting, connections, and is provided by the installing company. This third one is the most important and the one most often glossed over.

🚩 Red Flag Answer

"25-year warranty on everything.", This is almost never true as a blanket statement. Ask them to put the exact warranty terms in writing, covering each component separately. Vagueness about warranty is a major red flag.

✅ Right Answer

"Here is the breakdown in writing: 25-year panel performance warranty from [manufacturer], 10-year product warranty on panels, 5-year warranty on inverter, and a [X]-year workmanship warranty from us in writing." Specific, documented, and verifiable.

Why it matters: Many small solar companies in Vizag will not exist in 5 years. A workmanship warranty from a company that has closed down is worthless. Choose a company with roots deep enough in AP that they will still be here in 2051 to honour your warranty.

The subsidy process involves registering on pmsuryaghar.gov.in, submitting a DISCOM feasibility application, coordinating the APEPDCL net meter installation, attending the commissioning inspection, and uploading final documents for subsidy release. Q10 "What is your payment structure, and do you ask for 100% payment before installation?"

Your payment structure tells you everything about how much a company trusts its own work. A confident, established solar company with a track record in Visakhapatnam will accept a phased payment schedule, because they know the job will be done right and they are not worried about getting paid. A company that demands full payment before lifting a single panel onto your roof is either financially desperate, planning to disappear, or both.

The industry standard payment structure for solar installation is roughly: 30–40% advance to confirm the order and procure materials, 40–50% on installation day, and 10–20% after commissioning and system performance verification. This structure protects you, you are always paying for work already done or in progress, never paying ahead for something that has not happened.

🚩 Red Flag Answer

"We require full payment before we begin, because panels need to be ordered specially for you.", This is how solar advance fraud works. Once they have 100% of your money, your leverage disappears entirely.

✅ Right Answer

"We take 30% advance to confirm and order materials, 60% on installation, and 10% after commissioning and system performance verification." Phased payments, aligned with project milestones.

Why it matters: Visakhapatnam has seen a rise in advance fraud across industries in recent years. In solar specifically, paying 100% upfront to a company with no verifiable track record in Vizag is a serious financial risk. Always protect yourself with phased payments tied to verified progress.

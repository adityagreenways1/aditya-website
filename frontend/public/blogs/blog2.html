Planning a residential rooftop solar installation in Vizag and want to understand PM Surya Ghar Yojana subsidy? This guide explains the subsidy amounts, who is eligible, what documents are typically needed, and what the overall process looks like.

1) What is PM Surya Ghar subsidy?

PM Surya Ghar (for residential rooftop solar) is a Government of India initiative that provides financial support to help households reduce the upfront cost of solar. The subsidy is generally credited to your bank account after the system is installed and the required approvals are completed.

2) Subsidy amounts (commonly used slabs)

Subsidy is typically based on the approved solar capacity. Many vendors reference these common slabs:

System size Indicative subsidy Notes
1 kW ₹30,000 Residential rooftop only
2 kW ₹60,000 Residential rooftop only
3 kW and above ₹78,000 (capped) Cap may apply based on guidelines
Note: Exact amounts and rules can change over time. Your final eligible subsidy depends on the current program guidelines, system sizing, approvals, and documentation.

3) Who is eligible?

  • You should be a residential electricity consumer with a valid connection.
  • Your rooftop should have sufficient space and suitable shading conditions.
  • Bank account details are required for direct transfer of the subsidy.
  • System must be installed as per the program and utility requirements.

4) Typical documents checklist

  • Latest electricity bill and consumer number
  • Aadhaar / PAN (as required)
  • Bank account details for subsidy credit
  • Property ownership proof (in some cases)
  • Rooftop photos / site survey details

5) End-to-end process (simple version)

  • Site survey: roof area check, shading, cable route, and load assessment
  • System design: choose capacity (kW), inverter type, and layout
  • Application & approvals: submit required details and get approvals
  • Installation: mounting, wiring, inverter setup, safety checks
  • Testing & commissioning: system checks, net-metering steps (if applicable)
  • Subsidy credit: subsidy transfer after successful completion

6) How to choose the right system size

A good starting point is your monthly electricity bill and available roof space. As a rough guideline, higher monthly consumption usually benefits from higher capacity (within feasibility limits). Your vendor should provide a generation estimate and payback calculation.

Quick FAQs

Will I get subsidy for commercial solar?

PM Surya Ghar is primarily for residential rooftop solar. Commercial systems follow different policies.

Do I need net-metering?

For on-grid systems, net-metering (or similar) is usually required to export excess energy to the grid.

How long does installation take?

Many residential installations can be completed in a few days after approvals and material readiness.

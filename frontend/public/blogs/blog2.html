1. What is PM Surya Ghar Yojana? (Simple Explanation)

PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana (also called PM Surya Ghar Yojana) was launched by the Government of India to make rooftop solar energy accessible and affordable for ordinary Indian families. The name "Muft Bijli" literally means free electricity — and that is the central promise of the scheme.

Under this scheme, eligible residential households receive a financial subsidy of up to ₹78,000 to install a rooftop solar system. The subsidy amount is transferred directly to the bank account of the beneficiary — with no middlemen, no agents, and no cash payments involved. It is fully managed through the official government portal pmsuryaghar.gov.in and implemented with the support of the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE).

What Does the Scheme Actually Give You?

  • Financial subsidy of ₹30,000 to ₹78,000 based on your solar system size
  • Up to 300 units of free electricity per month from your solar panels
  • Ability to sell surplus electricity back to the grid through net metering, earning further bill credits
  • Access to low-interest solar loans from nationalised banks if additional financing is needed
  • 25+ years of reduced or zero electricity bills after installation
  • Increased property value — solar-equipped homes command 10–15% higher resale prices in AP

The scheme is active across all of Andhra Pradesh — including Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, Rajahmundry, Nellore, Tirupati, Kurnool, Anakapalli, Vizianagaram, Srikakulam, Eluru, Guntur, and every district in between.

3. Who is Eligible? Full Eligibility Criteria for AP Residents

The eligibility criteria for PM Surya Ghar Yojana are simple and straightforward. Most homeowners in Andhra Pradesh will qualify. Here is the complete checklist:

Basic Eligibility Requirements

  • You must be an Indian citizen
  • You must own the residential property where solar panels will be installed
  • Your property must have adequate rooftop space (minimum ~80 sq ft for a 1 kW system)
  • You must have an active electricity connection in your name with a valid consumer number
  • You must not have previously availed any solar subsidy under a central or state government scheme
  • Your rooftop must be reasonably shadow-free for at least 5–6 hours per day

Who Else Can Apply?

  • Apartment flat owners — can apply for individual units or common area installations through Residential Welfare Associations (RWAs)
  • Housing societies — can participate collectively for common area solar installations
  • Farmers — agricultural solar pump subsidies are available separately; contact us for details

⚠️ Important: The electricity connection must be in your name (matching your Aadhaar). If the bill is in a family member's name, you may need to transfer it first. Aditya Greenways will advise you during the free site survey if this applies to your situation.

7. Common Mistakes That Delay Your Subsidy

Many homeowners across AP have faced delays — sometimes months — in receiving their subsidy because of avoidable mistakes. Here are the most common ones to watch out for:

  • Using a non-approved vendor: You only receive the subsidy if you install through a vendor listed on the official pmsuryaghar.gov.in portal. Installing from an unregistered vendor — however cheap — disqualifies you from the subsidy entirely.
  • Name mismatch between documents: If the name on your electricity bill does not exactly match your Aadhaar card, your application will be delayed or rejected. Fix this before applying.
  • Using non-ALMM listed panels: The solar panels must be from the Approved List of Models and Manufacturers (ALMM) maintained by MNRE. Cheap, uncertified panels will fail the inspection.
  • Requesting a system size exceeding your sanctioned load: Your solar system capacity must be within your electricity connection's sanctioned load. Going too large will get your application rejected.
  • Skipping net meter installation: Net metering is mandatory for the subsidy. If you skip this step or delay it, your subsidy application cannot be processed.
  • Incorrect bank details on portal: A single digit error in your bank account number or IFSC code will cause the subsidy transfer to fail. Double-check everything before submission.
  • Duplicate applications: Submitting multiple applications or applying on behalf of someone else in the same household will trigger a flag and delay processing significantly.

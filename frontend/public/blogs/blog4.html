2. On-Grid Solar: How It Works, Pros, Cons & Who It's For

An on-grid solar system (also called a grid-tied system) is the simplest and most popular type of rooftop solar installation in Visakhapatnam. Here is exactly how it works:

Your solar panels generate DC electricity from sunlight. An on-grid inverter converts this DC power into AC electricity that your home appliances can use. When your solar system is generating more power than your home needs — for example, during the middle of a sunny afternoon — the surplus power is exported to the APEPDCL/APSDCL electricity grid through a net meter. These export units are recorded and credited against your future electricity bills, effectively earning you money back.

When your solar generation is lower than your consumption — at night, on cloudy days, or during peak demand — your home draws the remaining power from the grid as normal. The net meter tracks the difference, and you are billed only for the net units you consume beyond what you exported.

⚠️ Important safety note: On-grid inverters are designed to automatically shut down when the grid experiences a power cut. This is a mandatory safety requirement — it prevents your solar system from back-feeding live electricity into lines that APEPDCL technicians may be working on. This means an on-grid system will NOT power your home during a power outage, even in bright sunshine.

3. Off-Grid Solar: How It Works, Pros, Cons & Who It's For

An off-grid solar system operates completely independently from the public electricity grid. Your solar panels, a charge controller, a large battery bank, and an off-grid inverter form a completely self-contained power system. You generate power during the day, store it in batteries, and use it as needed — day and night — with no grid connection at all.

To be fully off-grid, your battery bank must be large enough to power your home through multiple cloudy days. This means off-grid systems require significantly larger battery capacity — and therefore significantly higher upfront cost — than on-grid or hybrid systems of the same solar panel capacity.

🔋 Battery sizing reality check: To reliably power a typical Visakhapatnam household (800–1000 units/month) through 2 to 3 cloudy days without grid backup requires a battery bank of 10 to 15 kWh capacity. At current lithium battery prices, this alone costs ₹2.5 lakh to ₹4 lakh — before factoring in panels, inverter, and installation.

4. Hybrid Solar: How It Works, Pros, Cons & Who It's For

A hybrid solar system gives you the best of both worlds. Like an on-grid system, it is connected to the public electricity grid and can export surplus power via net metering. But like an off-grid system, it also has a battery bank that stores excess solar energy for use when the grid is unavailable.

A hybrid inverter intelligently manages power flow between four sources: your solar panels, your battery bank, the grid, and your home's load. The smart logic typically works like this: solar energy powers your home first, excess charges the battery bank, any remaining surplus is exported to the grid, and when solar is insufficient, the battery supplies power before drawing from the grid. During a power cut, the system automatically switches to battery + solar to keep your home running.

Pros and Cons of Hybrid Solar in Vizag

✓ Advantages
  • Powers your home during grid outages (load-shedding protection)
  • Still connected to grid — net metering credits apply
  • Eligible for PM Surya Ghar subsidy (on the on-grid portion)
  • Battery can also be charged from grid during off-peak hours
  • Can be installed without batteries now, add storage later
  • Ideal for Vizag areas with 3–8 hours of daily power cuts
  • Protects sensitive electronics from outage-related damage
✗ Limitations
  • Higher upfront cost than on-grid (₹1.4L–₹2.1L more for battery bank)
  • Battery replacement cost every 8–12 years
  • Longer payback period than on-grid alone
  • More complex system with additional maintenance points
  • Subsidy only applies to the on-grid component, not batteries
🏙️
Ideal For

Visakhapatnam households in areas with 3 or more hours of daily load-shedding, homes with critical medical equipment, small businesses that cannot afford downtime, or homeowners who want energy security alongside bill savings.

6. Which Systems Qualify for the PM Surya Ghar Subsidy?

This is one of the most important questions when evaluating your solar options in Visakhapatnam, and the answer is clear:

  • On-Grid systems — Fully eligible. Get up to ₹78,000 directly into your bank account for a 3 kW or larger system
  • Hybrid systems — Partially eligible. The solar panel and on-grid inverter components qualify for the subsidy; the battery bank does not
  • Off-Grid systems — Not eligible. PM Surya Ghar Yojana requires a grid connection for subsidy approval

⚠️ Critical point for hybrid buyers: Your hybrid system must use a hybrid inverter that is also certified as a grid-tie inverter, and the overall system must meet DISCOM technical requirements. Some cheap hybrid systems sold in Vizag use non-ALMM-listed panels or uncertified inverters that fail the APEPDCL inspection — disqualifying you from the subsidy even on the eligible components. 9. Frequently Asked Questions

Which solar system is best for Visakhapatnam homes in 2026?
For most homes in urban and semi-urban Visakhapatnam, an on-grid (grid-tied) 3 kW solar system is the best choice. Does off-grid solar qualify for PM Surya Ghar subsidy in Andhra Pradesh?
No. The PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana subsidy is strictly for on-grid (grid-connected) rooftop solar systems. Off-grid systems, which operate independently from the electricity grid, are not eligible for the central government subsidy. This is a major financial disadvantage of off-grid systems and one of the main reasons they are rarely the right choice for residential properties in Visakhapatnam that already have a grid connection.
What is the cost difference between on-grid and hybrid solar in Visakhapatnam?
A 3 kW on-grid system costs approximately ₹1,32,000 after the PM Surya Ghar subsidy. A comparable 3 kW hybrid system with a 5 kWh lithium battery bank costs approximately ₹2,92,000 after the subsidy on the grid-connected components — a difference of about ₹1.6 lakh. Whether this is worth it depends on how frequently your area in Vizag experiences power cuts. Aditya Greenways can help you calculate the exact break-even point for your situation.
Can I add batteries to my existing on-grid solar system in Vizag?
Only if your inverter is a hybrid inverter that supports battery connectivity. Standard on-grid string inverters cannot accommodate batteries — you would need to replace the inverter, which adds significant cost. If you think you may want battery backup in the future, ask Aditya Greenways to install a battery-ready hybrid inverter from the beginning. This costs slightly more upfront but saves you the full inverter replacement cost later.
How many hours of power cuts does Visakhapatnam typically experience?
Power cut frequency varies significantly by area in Visakhapatnam. Established urban areas — MVP Colony, Madhurawada, Bheemunipatnam, and similar — typically experience 0 to 2 hours of cuts per day. Developing and peri-urban zones can see 3 to 6 hours daily during summer months. Parts of Anakapalli district can experience longer, less predictable outages. Our site survey team can advise you based on your specific address and our experience with your local APEPDCL substation.
Is hybrid solar worth the extra cost in Visakhapatnam?
It depends on your load-shedding frequency and what power cuts cost you. For homes with less than 2 hours of cuts per day, an on-grid system with a simple UPS backup is usually more cost-effective. For households with 3+ hours of daily outages, critical equipment like medical devices, or home businesses where downtime costs money — the hybrid premium pays for itself in avoided inconvenience and lost productivity. Our team at Aditya Greenways can calculate the breakeven point specifically for your energy usage and local grid reliability.
What is the lifespan of solar batteries in Visakhapatnam's climate?
Lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO4) batteries — which Aditya Greenways recommends for all hybrid installations — typically last 8 to 12 years in Visakhapatnam's coastal climate. Their performance is relatively stable across temperature ranges and they tolerate Vizag's humidity well. Lead-acid batteries last only 4 to 7 years and are not recommended for long-term hybrid systems. When budgeting for hybrid solar, plan for one battery replacement over the 25-year system lifespan.

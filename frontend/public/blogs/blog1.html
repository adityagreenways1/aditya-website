3. PM Surya Ghar Yojana: Get Up to ₹78,000 Subsidy in Visakhapatnam

The Government of India's PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana is a game-changer for residential solar adoption. Under this scheme, homeowners in Visakhapatnam and across Andhra Pradesh can receive direct subsidies credited straight to their bank accounts, making rooftop solar significantly more affordable.

Subsidy Amounts Under PM Surya Ghar Yojana

  • 1 kW system → ₹30,000 subsidy
  • 2 kW system → ₹60,000 subsidy
  • 3 kW and above → ₹78,000 subsidy (maximum cap)

Who is Eligible?

  • Residential households across Andhra Pradesh (including Visakhapatnam)
  • Must install through a PM Surya Ghar approved vendor like Aditya Greenways
  • System must be on-grid (connected to the electricity grid)
  • Installation must use ALMM-listed solar panels
